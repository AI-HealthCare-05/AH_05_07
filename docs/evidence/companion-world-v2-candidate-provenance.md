## Scope

candidate intake family only.

It applies to the eight immutable binaries listed below:

- species: `koala`, `mouse`, `pig`, `owl`
- variants: `lite`, `standard`
- candidate version mapping: `v002`

activation, runtime delivery approval, R2 publication, or scene qualification.
